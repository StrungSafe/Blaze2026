XOGrid mainnet spend probe

Real mainnet BCH. This moves one of your plain coins back to the same address. Only the 250 sat network fee (about $0.001) leaves your wallet. Coins carrying CashTokens are never touched. Selene shows the exact transaction for approval, and nothing is sent until you press Broadcast.

  1. Connect, then scan the QR code in Selene.
  2. Find coins, then click the coin to use (needs at least 796 sats).
  3. Sign and approve in Selene. The page checks the signed transaction itself.
  4. Broadcast, then copy the result log back to Nathaniel.
